# Agent instructions

## Third-party 3D assets

Before adding a model, record its author, source URL, and exact license. Assets whose
license permits source redistribution (for example CC BY) may be committed with their
attribution. Assets that may be embedded in the game but not redistributed stand-alone
must use the encrypted licensed-asset pipeline.

Read `docs/licensed-assets.md` before importing, replacing, decrypting, or re-encrypting
any licensed model. Never commit `.env` or anything under
`Assets/Resources/Generated/Licensed/`.
