# Texture Pack: Stylized 01

Author: Julio Sillet (JulioVII / Sillet 3D Art)  
Source: https://juliovii.itch.io/stylizedpk01  
Acquired: 2026-09-01  
Selected materials: Stylized 01 Bricks, Stylized 02 Stone Ground,
Stylized 03 Wood Planks and Stylized 04 Rocks  
Source format: 4K JPEG PBR maps  
License: Creative Commons Attribution (CC-BY), as stated by the publisher  

The publisher permits commercial applications and modifications, with attribution,
and prohibits reselling the texture pack as a standalone product. BROcoli combines
each source metallic and glossiness map into a Unity metallic/smoothness texture;
the Stone Ground base color is desaturated to turn its bright green moss into
neutral stone-and-gravel joints, and the Rocks base color is desaturated out of
its lilac cast into grey dungeon stone. The DirectX normal, ambient-occlusion, and
height maps are otherwise unchanged. The prop sets (Wood Planks, Rocks) are stored
at 2048 px; the floor and wall sets stay at 4K. `scripts/prepare_stylized_pbr_textures.py`
performs the conversion.
